# Evaluation Rubric

Each criterion is evaluated on a scale from 1 to 5.

| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Significant issues |
| 2 | Multiple areas for improvement |
| 3 | Acceptable performance |
| 4 | Good performance |
| 5 | Excellent performance |

## Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Naturalness

Evaluates whether the voice sounds fluid, natural, and appropriate for human conversation.

### 2. Clarity

Evaluates whether the speech is easy to understand and whether the message is communicated effectively.

### 3. Audio Quality

Evaluates technical aspects such as clarity of sound and the presence of noticeable audio issues.

### 4. Consistency

Evaluates whether the voice maintains a consistent tone, pace, and overall quality throughout the interaction.

### 5. User Experience

Evaluates the overall quality of the interaction from the user's perspective.

## Final Assessment

The evaluator should provide a brief written justification for each score and identify relevant strengths and opportunities for improvement.
