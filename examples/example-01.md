# Example 01 — Educational Voice Response

## Scenario

A user asks an AI voice assistant:

> "Can you explain how solar energy works?"

Two hypothetical AI-generated voice responses were evaluated using the framework.

## Evaluation Summary

| Criteria | System A | System B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Naturalness | 4 | 5 |
| Clarity | 5 | 4 |
| Audio Quality | 4 | 5 |
| Consistency | 4 | 5 |
| User Experience | 4 | 5 |

## Analysis

System A provides a clear and direct explanation. However, the delivery may feel slightly less natural in longer sentences.

System B demonstrates a more natural conversational flow and consistent delivery, resulting in a stronger overall user experience.

## Conclusion

System B achieved a higher overall evaluation due to its naturalness, consistency, and overall interaction quality.

This example is fictional and was created exclusively for portfolio demonstration purposes.
